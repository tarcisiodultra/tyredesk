TyreDesk is a modified distribution of RustDesk 1.5.0.
Upstream: https://github.com/rustdesk/rustdesk
Upstream commit: fada664df7a294d1d1a9ca3e7cd3637069122f17
Modifications: Tyresoles branding and unattended-access defaults.

RustDesk copyright notices and the GNU Affero General Public License in
LICENCE are retained. TyreDesk has no paid activation or subscription.
Third-party dependencies retain their respective licenses.

Distribute the corresponding modified source with these binaries. The
TyreDesk Windows workflow produces TyreDesk-source.tar.gz alongside the
binary artifacts, including the hbb_common submodule and build instructions.
Keep that source archive available to recipients and remote users.
